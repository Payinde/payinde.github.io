IT/OT Segmentation Lab

A planned lab for separating IT, OT, and DMZ networks using pfSense, Windows systems, OpenPLC/Modbus simulation, and monitoring between network zones.

  • In Progress
  • pfSense
  • OT
  • DMZ
  • Modbus

Objective

Design a small IT/OT lab that shows network segmentation, controlled traffic flow, firewall rules, and safe monitoring between business and industrial-style systems.

Planned work

Create IT, OT, and DMZ networks; deploy pfSense; add OpenPLC or a Modbus simulator; configure allow-list firewall rules; and capture permitted/blocked traffic.

Skills to demonstrate

Segmentation, firewall policy design, Modbus awareness, Windows/OT coexistence, network monitoring, and risk-based documentation.

Status

In progress. The page is prepared so screenshots, diagrams, rules, and findings can be added as the lab is completed.
